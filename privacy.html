Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who operates this application

"Sure Finance Personal" is a private, self-hosted instance of the open-source Sure personal finance software. It is operated by one private individual (the "operator") exclusively for managing the operator's own finances. It is not offered to third parties and has no other users.

2. Data processed

Through the Enable Banking API and PSD2 account information services, and only after the operator's explicit authorisation with their own bank, the application reads the operator's own account balances, account identifiers (such as IBAN) and transaction history. Access is read-only: the application does not initiate payments.

3. Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed solely for the operator's personal budgeting and household finance management (purely personal activity). No profiling, marketing, advertising or resale takes place.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server controlled by the operator and reachable only through a private VPN network (Tailscale). It is not published on the public Internet. Sensitive fields are encrypted at rest by the application, and access credentials are kept out of source control.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or disclosed to third parties, except for the technical exchange with Enable Banking and the operator's bank that is needed to retrieve the data.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the operator uses the application. The operator can revoke bank access at any time, through their bank or by disconnecting the connection in the application, and can delete all stored data.
